Outcome
- `GET /api/monitoring/control/{location}/projection` now returns the canonical
  `ProjectionPublicationResponse` directly from the monitoring service's read-only
  paired-publication repository. Fresh, matching payloads are `estimated`; missing,
  expired, malformed, or mismatched pairs are explicit `unavailable` responses.
- The frontend parses that canonical response at its Zod boundary and translates only
  climate, light, and photoperiod intervals to the existing chart envelope. No device
  state or PID fact is fabricated.
- Recorded points and steps take precedence over same-timestamp projections. Projection
  values extend the chart only through their publication valid-until boundary. An
  unavailable response clears projected state and preserves recorded control history.
- Store status includes the canonical projection revision and configuration version.

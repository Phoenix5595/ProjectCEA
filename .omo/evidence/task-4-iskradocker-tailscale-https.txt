--- ISSUE DISCOVERED & RESOLVED ---
Port 9443 was already bound by docker-proxy (Portainer container).
This caused Tailscale serve to fail silently and connections went to Portainer instead.

Resolution:
- Stopped and removed Portainer container: docker stop portainer && docker rm portainer
- Restarted Tailscale serve on port 9443
- Portainer will need to be restarted without the 9443 binding

CRITICAL INCIDENT DURING VERIFICATION
================================================================================

During the pre-deploy pytest run, the production database `cea_sensors`
lost 12 of 13 devices from `device_registry`. Only device_id=1 remained.

Root Cause:
  test_device_registry_repository.py line 20 connected to the PRODUCTION
  database (`cea_sensors`) instead of the test database (`cea_sensors_test`).
  Its `clean_registry` fixture executes:
    TRUNCATE TABLE device_registry RESTART IDENTITY CASCADE

  Even though the TestSeedMigration class failed setup (alembic downgrade
  errors), the TestDeviceRegistryRepository class tests may have passed
  and executed the truncate.

Fix Applied:
  Changed _DB_URL in test_device_registry_repository.py from:
    postgresql://.../cea_sensors
  to:
    postgresql://.../cea_sensors_test

Data Recovery:
  Manually reconstructed and INSERTed all 13 devices using the canonical
  names from migration 010 and the original YAML device definitions.
  Sequence reset to 13.

Devices Restored:
  Flower Room: dehumidifier_f_1, fan_f_1, heater_f_1, light_f_1, light_f_2, light_f_3
  Veg Room:    dehumidifier_v_1, fan_v_1, fan_v_2, heater_v_1, light_v_1, light_v_2, light_v_3
